Our Story

15 YEARS OF PRECISION.

James Hannah opened the doors of his Wichita shop in 2009 with a single Snap-On toolbox, a downdraft paint booth, and one stubborn promise: no shortcuts, no cheap parts, no compromises.

Today, we're a 14,000 sq ft I-CAR Gold Class certified facility — the highest training tier in the collision industry. We've repaired everything from family minivans to six-figure exotics.
